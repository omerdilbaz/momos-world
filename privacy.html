Momo's World – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Momo's World is a learning app for children. We built it to be safe for kids and simple for parents. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app stores

Child profiles (a first name or nickname you choose, an age and an avatar), progress, stars and stickers are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud or computer backups for your device, this data is included in your own backup, like any app's data. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also delete a profile at any time in the Grown-ups area.

What we do not do

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts, logins, chat or social features.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchases, settings and external links are behind a parental gate.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules one reminder on your device for 2 days before the trial ends. No data leaves the device.

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, no parental consent is required to use the app. If you believe any information has been collected, contact us and we will help right away.
